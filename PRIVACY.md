# WanderBites Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 29 July 2026**

WanderBites is a social food-discovery app. You follow people whose taste you
trust, and see where they recommend eating. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and how to get rid of it.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **exceldaily7@gmail.com**

---

## What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address, and if you sign in with Google,
the basic profile Google returns (name, email, profile picture). We use this
to create and secure your account.

**Profile information you choose to provide.** Display name, username, bio,
profile and cover photos, home city, taste tags, and taste personality. All of
this is optional except a display name and username, and all of it is visible
to other users by design — a WanderBites profile is a public thing.

**Content you create.** Recommendations, what-to-order notes, photos you
upload, lists, comments, saves, visits, and ratings you give to other people's
recommendations.

**Location.** With your permission, your approximate or precise location, used
to centre the map on you, show distance to restaurants, and rank nearby
suggestions. Location is used while you are using the app; we do not track you
in the background, and we do not store a history of where you have been. If
you decline the permission, the app still works — you can browse and search
anywhere in the world manually.

**Usage and diagnostics.** Anonymous analytics events (screens opened, actions
like saving a restaurant) and crash reports, through Google Firebase. These
help us find bugs and understand which features are used. They are not sold or
used to build advertising profiles.

**Device token.** If you enable notifications, a push token so we can deliver
them.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not show third-party advertising.
- We do not track your location in the background.
- We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

## Third parties we use

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, authentication, file storage | Account, profile, content, photos |
| Google Maps Platform | Map display, restaurant data, geocoding | Map coordinates you are viewing |
| Google Firebase | Analytics, crash reporting, push notifications | Anonymous usage events, crash data, push token |
| Brevo | Sending account emails (confirmation, password reset) | Your email address |

Restaurant information and photos shown in the app come from Google Maps
Platform and are subject to Google's terms. Photos posted by users are theirs.

## How long we keep it

Account and profile data is kept while your account exists. Analytics and
crash data are retained on Firebase's default schedules. When you delete your
account, see below.

## Your choices and rights

**Delete your account.** In the app: **Settings → Delete account**. This
permanently removes your profile, recommendations, photos, lists, saves,
visits and follows. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email **exceldaily7@gmail.com** from the address
on the account with the subject "Delete my WanderBites account" and we will
delete it within 30 days.

**Access or correct your data.** Most of it is editable in Edit profile. For
anything else, email us.

**Location.** You can revoke the location permission at any time in Android
Settings; the app degrades gracefully.

**Notifications.** Turn them off in the app's settings or in Android settings.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (for example under
GDPR or the CCPA), including access, correction, deletion, and portability.
Email us and we will honour them.

## Children

WanderBites is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect their information. If you believe a child has given us data, email us
and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your content is enforced at the
database level with row-level security, so users can only read and write what
they are entitled to. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this
seriously.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where
appropriate, notify you in the app.
