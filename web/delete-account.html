Delete your WanderBites account

App: WanderBites (com.wanderbites.app). Developer contact: exceldaily7@gmail.com.

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open WanderBites and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you have lost access to the app or cannot sign in, email us from the address on the account.

Email a deletion request

Subject: Delete my WanderBites account. Include your username if you know it. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and usually far sooner.

What gets deleted

All of the following is permanently removed, immediately:

  • Your profile: display name, username, bio, avatar and cover photo, home city, taste tags and taste personality
  • Every recommendation you published, including photos and what-to-order notes
  • Your lists and everything in them
  • Your saved restaurants and visits
  • Your follows, and other people's follows of you
  • Your comments, likes, and the accuracy ratings you gave to other people's recommendations
  • Your notifications and push token

Your sign-in

Deleting in the app removes everything above right away. The sign-in credential itself — the email address you log in with — sits in a shared authentication pool used by other apps from the same developer, so it is not removed automatically. If you want the login erased as well, say so in the email above and we will remove it within 30 days.
