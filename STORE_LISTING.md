## Full description (4000)

```
Most restaurant apps ask "what's the highest rated place nearby?"

WanderBites asks a better question: whose taste do you actually trust?

Follow food explorers — Tasters — whose palate matches yours, and see exactly
where they recommend eating. Every place on your map got there because a real
person vouched for it, not because it collected the most anonymous stars.


A MAP, NOT A DIRECTORY

Open the app and you get a map of the world, not an endless list. Pan
anywhere — your neighbourhood, a city you're visiting next month, somewhere
you've never been — and see the places worth eating, who recommends them, and
why.

Tap a pin for the essentials: the photo, the cuisine, the price, how far, and
the one line from a Taster explaining what makes it worth the trip.


BUILT ON TRUST, NOT VOLUME

Anyone can leave a review. Not everyone's opinion is worth the same.

After you visit somewhere because of a recommendation, you can say whether it
lived up to it. That feedback — from people who actually went — is what builds
a Taster's score. Likes don't count. Follower counts don't count. Only whether
your recommendations hold up.

The result: when someone with a high score tells you a place is special, that
means something.


BITESWIPE

Can't decide where to eat? BiteSwipe deals you a quick stack of nearby places,
ranked by the Tasters you trust and the food you actually like. Swipe right to
save, left to skip. Every card tells you why it surfaced — "Recommended by
someone you follow", "Hidden gem nearby", "Matches cuisines you like".

Everything you save lands straight back on your map.


PLAN A TRIP

Search any city on earth and the map goes there. Barcelona, Chiang Mai, the
town you grew up in. Build lists like playlists — Best Pizza in New York,
Tokyo Hidden Gems, Places Worth the Detour — and take them with you.


YOUR TASTE PROFILE

Show people how you eat: bold or delicate, mild or hot, casual explorer or
planner. Add taste tags, and find the people you overlap with. Your profile
becomes a living map of everywhere you've recommended, visited and saved.


WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Explore restaurants on a world map
• Follow Tasters whose taste you trust
• Save places and mark the ones you've visited
• Publish quick recommendations with photos and what to order
• Rate whether a recommendation was accurate
• Build and share lists
• Discover with BiteSwipe
• Track the cities and countries you've eaten your way through


Free to use. No ads.

WanderBites is new, so some profiles are clearly marked "Sample" to show how
the app works. Real Tasters are never labelled that way.
```
